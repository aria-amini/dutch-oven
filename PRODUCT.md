# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Home cooks — weeknight cooking, saving recipes, family meals. Food is care and
pleasure, not data entry.

## Product Purpose

dutch-oven is a recipe and food-tracking app. The primary job is finding and
cooking recipes; meal logging and planning support that loop. Success means
cooking from the app feels effortless and the act of logging a cooked meal is a
satisfying byproduct, not a chore.

## Positioning

One loop: find it, cook it, log it. Planning, cooking, and tracking are the same
motion, not three features bolted together.

## Operating Context

Used in the kitchen, often mid-cooking with wet hands; also at a desk when
planning the week. Responsive web app on the TanStack Start stack (React 19,
Vite+, Drizzle/Postgres, Better Auth, Tailwind v4, shadcn).

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed: recipe browsing/saving/cooking, meal logging (one-tap byproduct of
cooking, feeds cook-count ranking), import-from-URL (slot built, parser
pending), books (collections) as organization + AI scope, a home "what's
tonight" decision surface, groceries (list from picked recipes, later).
Decision-first, not calendar-first: week planning deferred. Undecided:
nutrition/macro detail depth, sharing/social features. Rejected scope: pantry
tracking (built, then removed 2026-08-14).

## Brand Commitments

Name: dutch-oven. User-pinned visual direction (2026-08-14, second revision):
neobrutalist kitchen — butcher-paper cream ground, cast-iron ink 2px borders and
hard offset shadows, bold ingredient colors (tomato #ff4b26, yolk #ffc21c, basil
#2fa84f, eggplant #5b3df5), extrabold uppercase labels, zero radius. Earlier
directions the user moved past: metro tiles (cyan/magenta/lime), and homey
pantry-shelf with mustard/ketchup accents (user reversed that pin). Explicitly
rejected: restaurant/diner ticket-rail world — too commercial, not homey.

## Evidence on Hand

None — template app, no real recipe content or imagery yet. Recipe data shown in
concepts is synthetic and must be labeled or replaced.

## Product Principles

- Cooking first: every surface serves getting dinner made.
- Logging is a byproduct of cooking, never a separate data-entry task.
- Warmth and personality over clinical precision.
